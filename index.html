🕯️ Candle Patterns Guide
Hover over any candle on the chart to see its name. Study these patterns — they're your entry signals.
📈 Bullish Patterns — Signal: Price going UP
🟢 Hammer
Small body at top, long lower wick (≥2× body). At the bottom of a move = buyers rejected lower prices hard. Strong buy signal at support.
🟢 Bullish Engulfing
Big green candle completely swallows the previous red candle. Buyers completely took over. One of the most powerful buy signals.
🟢 Bullish Pin Bar
Long lower wick (≥2.5× the body) with a small body near the top. Price shot down but buyers slammed it back up. Buy signal.
🟢 Bullish Marubozu
Big green candle with almost no wicks. Pure buyer momentum — nobody pushed back. Strong continuation signal upward.
🟢 Dragonfly Doji
Opens and closes at the top, long lower wick. Buyers rejected the lows completely. Very bullish, especially at support levels.
📉 Bearish Patterns — Signal: Price going DOWN
🔴 Shooting Star
Small body at bottom, long upper wick (≥2× body). At the top of a move = sellers rejected higher prices hard. Sell signal at resistance.
🔴 Bearish Engulfing
Big red candle completely swallows the previous green candle. Sellers completely took over. One of the most powerful sell signals.
🔴 Bearish Pin Bar
Long upper wick (≥2.5× the body) with a small body near the bottom. Price shot up but sellers slammed it back down. Sell signal.
🔴 Bearish Marubozu
Big red candle with almost no wicks. Pure seller momentum — nobody pushed back. Strong continuation signal downward.
🔴 Gravestone Doji
Opens and closes at the bottom, long upper wick. Sellers rejected the highs completely. Very bearish, especially at resistance.
⚖️ Neutral Patterns — Wait for Confirmation
⚪ Doji
Open and close are almost equal. Nobody won — bulls and bears fought to a draw. Watch the NEXT candle to see who wins.
⚪ Inside Bar
The candle's high and low fit completely inside the previous candle. Market is coiling up. Breakout coming — watch which direction it goes.
⚪ Spinning Top
Small body in the middle with wicks on both sides. Indecision. Market tried both directions and came back to center. Wait.
🏰 Supply & Demand Zones
Demand Zone (Buy Zone) — A price area where buyers stepped in before. Price shot UP from here in the past. When price returns to this level, buyers often step in again. Look to BUY at demand zones.
Supply Zone (Sell Zone) — A price area where sellers stepped in before. Price fell DOWN from here in the past. When price returns to this level, sellers often step in again. Look to SELL SHORT at supply zones.
The Rule: Don't buy at supply (resistance). Don't sell at demand (support). Wait for price to reach the right zone, then look for a candle pattern signal to enter.
📐 Trend Structure (HH/HL/LH/LL)
Uptrend = Higher Highs (HH) + Higher Lows (HL) — Each peak is higher than the last, and each pullback holds higher than the last. Price is making a staircase UP.
Downtrend = Lower Highs (LH) + Lower Lows (LL) — Each peak is lower than the last, and each pullback drops lower than the last. Price is making a staircase DOWN.
Pro tip: Toggle 📐 Structure on the chart to see HH/HL/LH/LL labeled automatically. Toggle 🔲 Zones to see Supply & Demand zones highlighted.
